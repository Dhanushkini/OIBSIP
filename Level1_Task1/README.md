# Level 1 Task 1: Retail Sales EDA & Business Insights

## 📌 Overview
This task focuses on performing advanced descriptive, demographic, time-series, and correlation analyses on retail operations to derive executive-level business strategies.

## 📊 Key Insights & Findings
* **Statistical Skew:** Identified a notable gap between Average Transaction Value ($456.00) and Median Order Value ($135.00), heavily driven by a dominant $50.00 unit-price mode.
* **Temporal Trends:** Uncovered structural demand spikes peaking aggressively during early October holiday shopping, outperforming late-November waves.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
1. **Product Bundling:** Create strategic checkout bundles (e.g., matching $50 "Clothing" items with $25 "Beauty" attachments) to lift the Average Order Value (AOV).
2. **Ad-Auction Optimization:** Reallocate core digital marketing capital to early October ahead of Black Friday/Cyber Monday price inflation.
